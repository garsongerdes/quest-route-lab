QUEST ROUTE LAB
===============

Research and development of data-driven game progression routes for World of Warcraft: Forever.

PURPOSE
-------
This repository is an early development workspace for researching quest progression and designing independently authored leveling routes. Initial research focuses on Horde leveling, starting with a small, testable level 1-12 route.

The intended in-game experience is deliberately simple: one clear instruction at a time. The addon provides information to the player; it does not automate gameplay.

RESEARCH AREAS
--------------
- Quest availability and faction requirements
- Quest prerequisites and chains
- Quest giver and turn-in locations
- Objective locations and travel distance
- Experience and reward information
- Route sequencing and in-game validation

DATA PROVENANCE AND ATTRIBUTION
-------------------------------
Third-party reference data will be tracked by source, applicable terms, verification status, and date accessed. Historical Classic data will not be treated as confirmed WoW: Forever data.

Where ForeverDB data is used or displayed, the project will follow ForeverDB's published API terms, including visible attribution. This project does not claim ownership of third-party data and will not resell or redistribute ForeverDB data as its own.

Commercial distribution of independently authored route material informed by third-party reference data will be evaluated against the applicable source terms before release.

DEVELOPMENT STATUS
------------------
Early research and prototype stage. Routes are not yet validated or suitable for release.

SCOPE
-----
The project is intended to provide player-facing guidance only. It will not implement automated movement, combat, botting, protected-interface bypasses, or unauthorized access to game memory or network traffic.
