# Quest Route Lab

Research and development of data-driven quest progression routes and an informational leveling-guide addon for World of Warcraft: Forever.

## Project purpose

The goal is to research quest progression and develop efficient, independently authored leveling routes. The initial development target is a small Horde level 1–12 route that can be tested in the game before expanding coverage.

The intended player experience is deliberately simple: one clear instruction at a time. The addon is informational; it does not automate movement, combat, or other gameplay.

## Research areas

- Quest availability and faction requirements
- Quest prerequisites and quest chains
- Quest giver and turn-in locations
- Objective locations and travel distance
- Experience and reward information
- Route sequencing and in-game validation

## Data provenance and attribution

Third-party reference information will be tracked by source, applicable terms, verification status, and date accessed. Historical Classic data will not be treated as confirmed WoW: Forever data.

Where ForeverDB information is used or displayed, the project will follow ForeverDB's published API terms, including visible attribution. This repository does not claim ownership of third-party data and will not resell or redistribute ForeverDB data as its own.

Any future distribution of independently authored route material informed by third-party reference data will be reviewed against the applicable source terms before release.

## Development status

Early prototype and research stage. The included addon is a user-interface proof of concept, not a completed leveling guide. No leveling route in this repository is represented as verified or optimized yet.

## Project boundaries

The project is intended to provide player-facing guidance only. It will not implement botting, automated movement or combat, protected-interface bypasses, or unauthorized access to game memory or network traffic.
